# The Coach

Speaking at a senior level: in committees, on boards, from the podium. Five parts.

- **Today**: a fifteen-minute session. Warm-up, the next step in the delivery course, a lexicon set (due reviews, then eight new terms), one meeting scenario, and either a speech-craft lesson or a rehearsal of the speech you're writing.
- **Lexicon**: 1,371 terms in 17 fields, each with a precise gloss, the sentence you would say, and a note on when it lands and when it sounds affected. Fields: meeting procedure and chairing; board governance and fiduciary duty; university governance; curriculum, assessment and standards; education policy and research; pedagogy and learning science; finance, budgets and audit; strategy, leadership and change; argument, reasoning and evidence; rhetoric and persuasion; high register; politics, negotiation and diplomacy; arts, music and cultural policy; law, ethics and employment in education; academic discourse and stance; the professional musician's vocabulary; plus The Floor's phrases. Learn in sets of eight with spaced review; Say it, or Use it in a live contribution with the meter running.
- **Meetings**: 22 moves that shape a committee of clever people (Framing, Testing, Building, Dissenting, Procedure, Closing). Each is taught, with phrasings at academic register and the versions to avoid, then practised against 132 realistic situations: SCSA committees, academic boards, school council, festival board, the principal's office, funding panels. You hear what was just said, think for twelve seconds, speak, then compare with two strong contributions.
- **Speeches**: 16 craft lessons (controlling idea to delivery, classical arrangement, writing for the ear, figures, numbers, memory, rehearsal); 12 occasions, each with its shape and an annotated model speech; 16 great speeches taken apart for their architecture; 80 openings and closings; and a workshop to draft your own speech part by part and rehearse it against the clock with the meter.
- **Course**: the delivery course, 30 units across Voice, Train of thought, Words, Body and Tone, rewritten at senior level. Each unit teaches, drills and applies.

Gym (free practice) and Progress are on Today.

## The meter
Pauses, longest pause, and rising or falling phrase ends are measured on the phone. Words per minute, fillers and the transcript use Chrome's speech recognition, which sends audio to Google; switch it off in Settings. Recordings stay on the phone. Not yet tested on the S24; if the transcript drops while recording is on, turn off Keep takes in Settings.

## Publish
Public repo `the-coach` (or replace the files in it if you already uploaded v1). Upload everything in this folder with `index.html` at the top level: `index.html`, `app2.js`, `content.js`, `curriculum.js`, `material.js`, `meter.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icons/`, this README. Settings > Pages > main / root. Install from Chrome (or Samsung Internet if Chrome's install record is stuck).

## Check before citing
Writers named only sources they were confident of and kept section numbers out where unsure. Worth a check before you quote them in a meeting: any statute section or threshold (Associations Incorporation Act tiers, special resolution thresholds), the WA privacy position, and the speech-rate ranges (given as working ranges, not research norms). Example figures and names in model speeches and scenarios are invented illustrations.

## Updating
Change `VERSION` at the top of `sw.js` whenever any file changes.
